# Company Capabilities Statement — MOCK DATA

> MOCK / FICTIONAL. "Nimbus Digital Services Pvt. Ltd." is an invented company used only to
> demonstrate the RFP Intelligence Platform. None of these facts describe a real organisation.

## Legal entity and registration
Nimbus Digital Services Pvt. Ltd. is a private limited company incorporated in 2012 under the Indian Companies Act, 1956. The company holds valid GST registration, PAN and TAN, and is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

## Office locations
Registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Delivery centres in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune. A client-facing branch office operates in Gurugram, Haryana. We do NOT have a registered or branch office within New Delhi city limits.

## Years of experience
The company has 13 years of continuous experience in web design, web application development, website maintenance and cloud services for government and enterprise clients.

## Financial standing
Average annual turnover over FY 2019-20 to FY 2023-24 was INR 42 crore. Net worth has been positive in every financial year since FY 2014-15. Audited balance sheets are available for the last five financial years.

## Headcount
1,150 full-time employees on payroll, including 640 software engineers, 85 UI/UX designers, 120 cloud and DevOps engineers and 60 information-security professionals.

## Quality certifications
ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management. CMMI Level 3 (Development) appraised by the CMMI Institute, valid until March 2027. We do NOT hold a CMMI Level 5 appraisal.

## Information security certifications
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified Information Security Management System covering all delivery centres, valid until 2027. ISO 22301 business continuity certification for the Bengaluru centre.

## Government web projects
Completed 9 website design and development projects for central and state government ministries between 2018 and 2024, each with an order value above INR 10 lakh, including portals for two state tourism departments and one central ministry event portal.

## Accessibility and GIGW compliance
All public-sector websites are built to Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and WCAG 2.1 Level AA. In-house accessibility testing with screen readers is part of every release.

## Security audits
Every government website is security-audited by a CERT-In empanelled auditor before go-live, and all high and critical findings are closed before launch. We run OWASP Top 10 testing in CI.

## Hosting and cloud
We deploy on MeitY-empanelled cloud service providers with data centres located in India. We hold partner status with two MeitY-empanelled CSPs. We do NOT operate our own data centre.

## Availability and support
Standard managed-service SLA is 99.9% monthly uptime with 24x7x365 monitoring and an L1/L2/L3 support desk. A 99.95% SLA is offered with an active-active multi-zone design. We do NOT commit to 99.99% uptime.

## Data protection
Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are managed in the cloud provider's KMS. Access follows least-privilege with MFA for all administrators.

## Content management systems
Expertise in Drupal, WordPress and headless CMS (Strapi) for government portals, including multilingual content in Hindi and English and Unicode support for Indian languages.

## Event and registration portals
Built and operated online delegate registration, accreditation and event-information portals for large conventions with up to 50,000 registered users, including payment gateway integration.

## Project delivery and timelines
Typical website design-to-go-live timeline is 90 to 120 business days. Project mobilisation starts within 10 working days of a work order.

## Commercial terms we accept
We can furnish an Earnest Money Deposit and a performance bank guarantee of up to 10% of contract value. Payment terms of milestone-based or quarterly in arrears are acceptable.

## Partnering and subcontracting
We deliver all work in-house and do not rely on subcontractors or consortium partners for website projects.

## Legal and compliance record
The company has never been blacklisted or debarred by any central or state government body or PSU, and has no pending litigation with any government department.

## Services we do NOT provide
We do NOT provide hardware supply, physical data-centre build-outs, telecom network installation, or on-site manpower outsourcing without a software delivery scope. We do NOT provide unlimited liability; our standard liability cap is 100% of contract value.
